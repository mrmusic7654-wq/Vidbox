## MOTION GRAMMAR (for the editor)

- **Shot length:** 4–8s standard; 3–4s in chapters 7–8 (horror set pieces); slow 8s pushes in the Buffett interlude.
- **Camera:** one move per shot — slow push-in, pull-out, or lateral pan. Nothing static, nothing wild.
- **Pop-ins:** every icon enters with overshoot (110% → 100% spring). Numbers count up with a deep boom on landing.
- **Stamps:** red rubber stamps ("EXIT", "SEALED BY LAW", "EXTENDED") slam in with 2-frame screen shake + thud.
- **Arrows:** draw themselves along their path, red, with a subtle drop shadow.
- **SFX palette:** cash cha-ching, paper rip, coin clink, low boom on stats, whoosh on transitions, ticking under chapter hinges.
- **Music:** tense neutral synth pulse, ducked −12dB under VO; drops out entirely on "Follow the money." (final chapter) and swells at the CTA.
- **Palette lock:** bg `#E8F1F5` + halftone dots · crimson `#D94A3D` · money green `#3FA34D` · navy `#1F3A5F` · gold `#F2B134` · highlighter yellow for key words.
